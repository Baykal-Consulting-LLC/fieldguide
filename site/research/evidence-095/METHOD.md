# Fresh-start question-count benchmark 0.95

All comparisons start from a new state. Only saved lifestyle choices and the profile's prescribed official 60-item responses are loaded. No prior field, career, facet, history, selected occupation, or previously supported match is loaded into matching or targeting.

Two fixed policies run on both 1,000-model cohorts at budgets 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 36, and 60 extra questions. The earlier 0.94 selector is extended beyond twelve for a matched-budget comparator. Its twelve-question Top lists must reproduce the published 0.94 result for every model. The new policy retains the initial activity lead, favors shared activity questions that inform multiple eligible careers, avoids redundant activity domains, and finishes promising specific evidence. It uses observed answers and correlations only, never latent profile facts, desired education, demographic attributes, income targets, or former Top careers. An unfamiliar required check defers a branch without becoming rejection; alternative responsibility routes remain available.

The exact reviewed 0.93 structured answer resolver supplies all simulated replies. Missing facts remain unknown. No career inventory, official question wording, support gate, education definition, or live student flow changes. Every unknown and unbound question consumes budget. Multi-select options are answered together; one question counts once regardless of how many options are selected. Stop when there is no eligible unanswered check; a budget is a maximum rather than a requirement to fill it.

A research-only evidence refresh avoids redoing invariant official scores at every question. Every published checkpoint is compared with the canonical 0.90 engine for occupation order, support, specific/task domains, and contrary evidence. No approximation is accepted in published results.

The archived 895/792 result used a median 143 distinct stored follow-up IDs (range 8–419), plus official60. Grok's archived 769/657 result used a median 195 (range 172–344), plus official60. These are synthetic stored response sets accumulated across research studies, not observed students completing one questionnaire. Career scopes of the same item ID count once. Rewrites and clicks cannot be reconstructed from this count. Lifestyle choices are separate. Item counts are not measured completion time; a multi-select item may take longer than a Likert item.

139 of the first cohort's 895 previously covered models lack typed preference facts to reproduce detailed replies safely. They retain unknown follow-up responses in this experiment. The original and fresh holdout splits are wholly untyped and are not evaluable for the typed-answer simulation. The all-model denominator is still shown for transparency, alongside typed-profile counts. Fixed development/holdout membership is preserved and reported separately. Prior holdout results have informed development; they are not unseen final validation.

Recovery has two meanings and is explicitly separated: previously covered models receiving any fresh Top, and models recovering an identical former Top career. Archived matches are used only for post-run recovery diagnostics, never to choose a question or grant evidence. College/graduate Top uses the existing bachelor's/graduate route definition, separate from certificates and associate routes. Top remains capped at three; supported degree options outside Top are also counted.

Research results do not establish real-student usefulness, test reliability, or the occupational validity of all authored simulator bindings. The live flow remains unchanged while these tradeoffs are reviewed.

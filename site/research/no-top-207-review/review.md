# Review of 207 models with exploration candidates and no supported Top

The largest opportunities are missing simulator activity bindings and better follow-up selection using existing specific-interest evidence. No matching rule, answer, profile, budget or published result was changed during this review.

The audit reproduces the live dashboard's exact 207 IDs from its 1,000 public model rows and replays their current evidence. The population is 160 development, 4 original holdout, 1 fresh holdout and 42 expanded holdout models. Of these, 178 are procedural synthetic cases and 29 are authored narrative profiles. The other 21 public models without Top or exploration candidates are outside this request.

## 1. Complete activity-choice bindings before changing Top requirements

There are 441 activity-choice options in the non-route question bank; 98 have no structured simulator binding. Real students can select these options. The current simulator calls an absent binding unfamiliar and cannot select the option from profile evidence.

Twenty-seven target models have a career with both defining interests already supported and fewer than the two required task contexts. Twenty-five are procedural models directly using the binding policy; two are authored cases with a similar saved-answer gap. Twenty-three of the 27 have a bachelor/graduate-route option (22 of the 25 procedural cases). Fifteen missing activity option IDs occur in these cases.

Examples include Interpreters and Translators, Public Relations Specialists, Exercise Physiologists, Market Research Analysts and Social Science Research Assistants. Missing choices include checking words and meaning, finding words for an audience, coaching recovery or a developing skill, and researching a question to defend a recommendation.

Complete explicit option-ID bindings only where the frozen topic/method facts actually support the choice. Keep other answers unfamiliar. Preserve the requirement for distinct activity contexts and independent defining interests. Filling a binding does not establish that every affected model will answer positively. The two authored cases require new recorded answers rather than retrospective rewriting.

## 2. Surface partially supported careers even below the broad-correlation cutoff

104 models have a bachelor/graduate profession outside the strong-correlation exploration pool with two eligible positive defining checks, one unresolved check, and no recorded specific No, review hold or explicit negative structured requirement. Of these, 75 are development models and 29 expanded holdout models.

For 52 models, at least one such profession is also outside the first 12 displayed options; for 26, every such profession is outside that first window. The full 457-career explorer remains accessible, but these careers are absent from the full-matrix exploration selector because it currently admits only supported careers or broad correlations of at least 0.608.

A safe discovery change is to offer a distinct 'promising specific evidence — one question remains' invitation alongside broad-profile candidates. Keep its evidence and broad-profile disagreement visible. Retain every Top confirmation requirement. Some of these profiles have weak or negative broad correlations, so two positive inferred checks need semantic review rather than automatic promotion.

Examples: Validation Engineers for scale056-0217 (broad r 0.413) and scale056-0225 (r 0.599); Soil and Plant Scientists for scale056-0023 (r 0.467); Environmental Engineers for scale056-0042 (r 0.321). These are question opportunities, not endorsed career assignments.

## 3. Resolve specific unknowns with neutral structured questions

Within the current strong-candidate pool, 37 models have an explicitly subject-aligned college-route candidate with two supported defining checks and exactly one unresolved check. All 37 involve a bachelor/graduate route. The unresolved response is unfamiliar in 33 cases and unsure in 4: these questions already exist and were presented. Repeating them or inventing Yes answers would not solve the information gap.

The 37 and the 104 outside-pool cases overlap in 18 models, giving 123 distinct models with at least one such one-check opportunity. This is not a forecast of 123 new Top matches. The task-context group overlaps these groups too; none of the opportunity counts should be summed.

Use short activity examples and independent response options: 'I would enjoy trying', 'Maybe', 'Not for me', 'I need an example'. Ask about interest in learning the process; prior experience is a separate question.

| Evidence to measure | Neutral example question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Dependable results and follow-through | Would you enjoy keeping a clear record of failed tests and checking a revised version before other people rely on it? | Electronics and validation engineering have subject/method evidence but an unstated separate safety/responsibility preference. |
| Comparing designs | Would you enjoy making two versions of something and testing which change improves it? | Measures revision interest across design, science, engineering and practical work. |
| Careful interpretation | Would you enjoy comparing two versions of a message and revising the words until their meaning is preserved? | Translation and communication need evidence beyond liking essays or helping with forms. |
| Composing versus directing a group | Would you enjoy making a short tune and revising it after listening back? Separately: would you enjoy guiding a group performance? | Composing and group coordination are alternative work patterns, not interchangeable prerequisites. |
| Clinical care versus scientific investigation | Would you choose to investigate why a result differs from a prediction, support someone directly through care, both, or neither? | Biology or diagnostic curiosity does not establish patient-care preference. |
| Managing commitments | Would you enjoy checking that an event's arrangements and spending match what was promised? | Event planning already has subject/method Yes answers for gap-021; this remaining responsibility is still unsure. |

Among models with a clean subject-aligned college candidate, absent structured dimensions include safety/dependability in 25, design in 21, precision in 19, leadership in 17, testing in 16 and patient care in 14. These counts overlap. Use balanced examples across arts, health, community, business, science and technical work rather than a new engineering-heavy block.

## 4. Audit construct-to-question assumptions and role alternatives

The Music Directors and Composers method question asks about developing musical ideas and revising by listening. Its simulator binding requires both expression and coordination. That can unnecessarily require the conductor/group-director pattern of a composer. scale056-0276 explicitly has sound and expression interests but no stated coordination preference; its other two checks are already Yes. Four other models have a related composer question opportunity, but some also lack expression evidence, so removing coordination alone cannot justify all five.

The purchasing comparison question is bound to organization plus persuasion, even though comparing supplier proposals does not by itself demonstrate or require persuading another person. Audit this and similar broad role-level bundles against the exact question being answered. Use explicit stable IDs and reviewed constructs, never prompt or biography keyword matching. Preserve independent evidence; do not let several reworded questions manufacture breadth from one preference.

All 430 named occupations have structurally confirmable question paths. The other 27 catalog entries are aggregate 'All Other' categories. More titles alone would not resolve these missing answers. Audio production remains a concrete inventory exception worth reviewing: sound engineering is currently only a discovery preview rather than a fully confirmable profession.

## 5. Preserve grounded alternatives and genuine uncertainty

The authored-profile review below retains every chosen interest without using demographics or mental-health descriptions as career evidence. Particularly clear missed directions are sound/weather for life-006, robotics/testing for life-038, electronics/mechanisms for life-094, and astronomy/dance for life-056. These call for relevant optional comparisons, not automatic clinical or college-teaching recommendations.

Sparse profiles such as gap-013 and gap-016 still justify low-pressure activity trials and an honest absence of a Top recommendation. new049-001's sole strong-correlation candidate is Coaches and Scouts with a recorded No; examine partial business evidence below the cutoff rather than overriding that No.

## Recommended sequence

1. Repair the 15 demonstrably missing activity-choice bindings in the task-gap cases, then review the other 83 unbound choices. Record positive, negative and still-unknown outcomes separately.
2. Add a separate specific-evidence follow-up lane regardless of the broad-correlation threshold; retain contradictions and broad-profile disagreement.
3. Add targeted neutral preference questions and review the composer/purchasing role bundles. Keep college and non-college choices available together with earnings.
4. Evaluate the global changes on development cases before a versioned holdout evaluation. Preserve all earlier profiles and answers. Current holdout aggregates and these diagnostic cases have been seen; they are not pristine final validation sets.

## Scope and evidence limits

This is an implementation-author review of synthetic profiles and current code, not independent counselor validation or observed student outcomes. The script inspected all candidate evidence for all 207 models, including partially supported alternatives in the full catalog, and the author separately reviewed all 29 authored profiles. The inferred Yes answers remain simulation assumptions. No improvement in supported Top coverage has been claimed or applied.

Reproduce with `node scripts/review-no-top-207.mjs` followed by `node scripts/summarize-no-top-207.mjs`. The scripts verify unchanged input/engine hashes and reconcile the exact IDs with the live source receipt. See `research/no-top-207-review/audit.json.gz`, `supplement.json`, `summary.json`, and `authored-profile-review.json`.

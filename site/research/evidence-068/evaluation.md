- Only four profession rules are tested. Of 172 authored profiles, only four have the previously frozen typed source annotations. The other authored interests remain unencoded, not absent. The 828 structured profiles can supply only scoped mathematics facts under these rules.

- Holdout splits remain separate. Earlier profiles and aggregate outcomes have been inspected, so these are diagnostic holdouts, not pristine validation.
- Known Yes/No answers and review holds are preserved. A retained No can block a new connection; the conflict remains for review.
- Earnings and budgets are unchanged. Missing pay stays unassessed. Bachelor / graduate means a recognized college or graduate catalog route; associate programs remain separate.

All 1,000 baseline Top lists and complete supported sets matched the current replay before applying the frozen rules. No 0.66 per-model profession-answer overrides were used.

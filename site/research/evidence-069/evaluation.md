## Findings

The broader rules add 256 specifically supported career claims in 28 professions compared with the four-rule benchmark. Seventeen additional models gain Top and 23 gain bachelor/graduate Top; no model loses either measure. Graduate Top grows 139 → 152 (14 gains, one displacement below the display cap). Lifestyle Top grows 239 → 250 (13 gains, two display displacements). All previously supported alternatives remain available.

Of the 256 new qualifications, 255 come from the 828 structured synthetic profiles; one additional supported alternative (Biostatisticians for Ari) comes from original saved choices. All newly Top-covered and degree-Top-covered models are structured profiles. No profile was enriched or its missing interests guessed. This is evidence about simulator and binding coverage, not independently measured student fit.

The principal repairs are contextual method facts, conjunction-versus-alternative errors, and distinguishing careful communication from research-program leadership. All 28,472 stored known profession-answer records and 89 held records were checked unchanged. Sixty-seven new-rule decisions encountered a recorded No and retained it. Missing specialty and clinical interests remain unknown.

## Source checks and limits

Existing question text and occupation sources are retained in inventory-audit.json. Selected primary-source checks confirmed the distinctions used in the review:
- [Biostatisticians](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/15-2041.01): biological/health analysis, defensible analytical plans and reporting.
- [Electronics engineers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/17-2072.00): design and product testing are distinct but connected activities.
- [Atmospheric scientists](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/19-2021.00): forecasting/model analysis and research are distinct occupational activities.
- [Biological-science postsecondary teachers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/25-1042.00): course preparation, evaluation and student assessment matter; explaining a subject alone is insufficient.

These source checks support occupational meaning, not the psychometric validity of new inference rules. The evidence alternatives remain authored research hypotheses.

- This is a research replay of designed synthetic cases, not observed student fit, measured aptitude or independent validation of these semantic rules.
- The full 404-career inventory was screened. Question-level alternative evidence was authored only for the listed careers; untouched careers are not certified as well calibrated.
- Concrete profile facts are distinct from broad topic ratings. Authored profiles can use only eligible saved activity IDs, topic ratings and the previous frozen annotations. No profession Yes is reversed into a new interest. No biographies, job titles, demographics, diagnoses, earnings or degree ambitions classify answers.
- Known Yes/No answers and review holds remain. Even an inferred No caused by an older binding is not silently overwritten. Retained conflicts need a fresh direct answer in a future study.
- Clinical care, specialist features, financial risk, legal representation, adult teaching and assessment, and explicit safety responsibilities are not inferred from broad subjects.
- Proposed evidence alternatives are hypotheses, not new validated scales or newly administered student questions. New simulated answers have medium confidence.
- Development and all three previously inspected holdouts use the same frozen rules, with separate reporting. These are diagnostic holdouts, not pristine final validation.
- Support gates, scores, ranking, education routes, budgets and earnings are unchanged. First paid stage values are planning proxies; missing pay remains unassessed. Route groups overlap.
- All earlier supported alternatives remain available. A new Top-three order can still displace a budget-covering option; coverage gains and losses are reported rather than hidden.

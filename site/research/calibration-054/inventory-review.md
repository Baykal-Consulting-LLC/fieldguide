# Calibration 054: 13 inventory-gap decisions

AI research audit, checked 2026-10-20. This is not counselor or psychometric validation. The two CareerOneStop outputs identify titles to audit; their fit bands do not establish that a title belongs in the product or matches a biography.

Read-only import of `explorerCatalog` from `dist/open-explorer.js` confirmed 457 active roles and no exact SOC for any of the 13 audited gaps. Related roles were checked in the same active catalog. Seven arts occupations warrant optional discovery content; four managerial/supervisory destinations belong in later pathway content; two require scope decisions. This is a content recommendation, not a runtime expansion or automatic Top promotion.

Nominees are limited to the three calibration-054 biography partitions. This audit deliberately uses development, legacy and supplement cases only; no original holdout nominee is used, and fresh holdout is excluded. Exact biography phrases are attached. Empty nominee arrays are deliberate where evidence is absent. Body appearance, demographics, trauma and family occupation are never nomination grounds.

Education statements distinguish BLS typical routes from O*NET respondent schooling and generic Job Zone descriptions. Neither a generic Job Zone nor college-oriented product scope establishes a universal bachelor’s requirement. Professional experience is not necessary to explore an interest, while genuine advancement roles need a clearly described earlier stage.

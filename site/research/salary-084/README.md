# Complete salary display and compact mobile choices (084)

Every one of the 457 catalog professions now resolves to a positive initial qualified-role pay estimate and 5/10/20-year planning scenarios. The 117 existing salary timelines remain unchanged; 340 expanded timelines reuse reviewed pay evidence or cached official TWC 2025 entry and median wages. Explicit SOC aggregation crosswalks are recorded in coverage.json. Where Austin and statewide data are suppressed, an identified Texas metro or a researched BLS national occupation median is used. The five national source pages were checked October 21, 2026; reported wage periods are preserved.

When no lower-paid-worker proxy exists, the initial estimate is explicitly modeled at 68% of the sourced median. Later scenarios use the existing product convention of 45%, 75%, and 100% of the gap toward the median, with no decline below the initial estimate. These are assumptions, not empirically established starting salaries or longitudinal forecasts. Geography, occupation aggregation and source URLs remain available in the UI.

The display layer does not change the frozen salary progression, expanded-pay evidence, matching rules, or first-paid-stage budget eligibility. Unsupported contextual pay does not manufacture a supported Top or an assessed lifestyle match. Board comparisons illustrate the displayed qualified-role scenario, whereas research metrics retain their existing first-paid-stage definitions.

The automatic and optional structured question forms now share compact checkbox rows with full-row labels, normal 24px controls, visible checked/focus states and two-column phone actions. Their responses and question wording are unchanged.

Guardrails, definitions and limits
  • Strong match: career-specific support under the unchanged matching rules. Degree coverage means a bachelor or graduate route among the first three strong matches. Associate and certificate-only routes are separate.
  • The shortlist preserves all confirmed careers. Unconfirmed priorities need recorded positive evidence, broad alignment at the existing 0.608 threshold and no contrary answer. This screen does not establish a match.
  • Only observed responses inform selection. Profiles are consulted by the unchanged response simulator after a question is selected. Missing interests stay unknown, and unknown responses count toward the 24-item budget.
  • One candidate selector was frozen before the run. Its question-availability estimates use prior development traces only. Holdouts were not used to tune this experiment; previously inspected holdouts are no longer pristine unseen validation.
  • Broader low-scoring connections remain browsable. The correlation threshold is not a probability or a validated shortlist-quality cutoff.
  • These outcomes measure synthetic coverage and allocation, not real student satisfaction, retention or predictive accuracy. Neither prototype was edited, built or published.

# Approach 3 research archive

Preserved research supporting a separate combined prototype. These files do not
change Career Explorer's student flow, matching rules, dashboard, or live Site.
Only public fictional profiles and their simulated answers are included.

- `shortlist-study-2026-10-24/report.html`: shorter results list and focused
  follow-up test; the tested focused selector regressed and was rejected.
- `protected-confirmations-2026-10-25/report.html`: protected confirmation test;
  every prior lost college connection returned, but student-level coverage did
  not improve over the current control. The report preserves the bookkeeping
  option lost by the extra scheduling rule.
- `design-sources/`: proposed route overview, actual-model mockups and simple
  clarification designs. These are design evidence, not validated new matches.
- `manifest.json`: original locations, exact SHA-256 hashes and file sizes.

Frozen source, question histories and input hashes remain unchanged. Original
replay scripts use the parent workspace's `outputs/` and `site/` layout; restore
the archived folders to those original paths to reproduce that run, rather
than silently rewriting a frozen protocol. Reports are self-contained HTML.
The diagram sources are conversation fragments and retain their original
presentation requirements. No credentials, live accounts or owner-only model
records were copied.

The combined prototype is developed in a separate `approach-3/` repository and
Site. A college pathway connection never establishes a career Strong match;
all education routes, unknown answers, source licenses and holdout splits must
remain explicit.

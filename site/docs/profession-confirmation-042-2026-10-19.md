# Defining-work confirmation and model rerun · 0.42

Top matches now require two profession-specific work interests alongside the existing whole-profile, repeated task, field, clinical-responsibility and route safeguards. These follow-ups ask whether the person wants to learn the regular work; they do not require prior training or measure aptitude. The activities are attributed occupational tasks, selected to include defining work rather than generic support duties. Tattoo prompts are explicitly editorial because occupation-specific task data is unavailable.

Shared task answers still open an opportunity. Missing, unfamiliar or uncertain defining-work responses leave it under **Needs confirmation**. Explicit dislike is identified separately. A profile-inferred Yes with medium or low confidence also remains provisional; a direct student response can resolve that uncertainty. This rule applies to all education routes. Pay and degree preference cannot establish interest.

Ranking compares the breadth of independent supporting question groups before using education preference and first-paid-stage pay among close candidates. A focused four-question check no longer receives a percentage advantage over broader field evidence. Unasked work stays unknown. When specific electrical work is confirmed, an overlapping mechanical comparison cannot prevent electrical work from being considered on its own evidence.

## Frozen replay

All 154 fictional models were rerun: 148 public and six owner-only. The original histories, official interest responses, previous work answers, budgets and 0.41 comparison answers remain unchanged. Three separate authors received biographies and exact core activity questions, without engine mappings, rankings, manual review verdicts or prior outcomes. They authored 1,310 task responses for 655 profession checks, covering the previous Top offers and the initially offered comparisons. Additional adjacent roles without authored core answers remain unconfirmed. Outputs and input-bank hashes were frozen before the new matching run.

These are simulated responses, not observed student choices. Biographies cannot resolve every work interest. Author confidence is an uncertainty label, not a calibrated probability; unresolved options require the actual student’s confirmation.

## Results

For the 39 reviewed models, the previous Top lists contained 103 offers: **27 remain Top and 76 need confirmation**. Of the 45 professions newly promoted in 0.41, **7 remain Top and 38 need confirmation**. The seven retained promotions are elementary teaching for Theo and Aisha, interpreting/translation for Rojin and Lucía, administrative assistance for Devin, bookkeeping for Sofía, and stock/order work for Noah. The new graduate promotions remain provisional.

Across all 154 models, 97 of 362 previous Top offers remain Top and 265 need confirmation. There are 79 models with at least one supported Top match. Lack of a supported Top does not erase related careers or establish that no suitable career exists.

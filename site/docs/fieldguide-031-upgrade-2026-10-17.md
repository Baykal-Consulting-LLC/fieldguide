# Fieldguide 0.31 · complete guidance upgrade

Reviewed 17 October 2026. Matching engine 3.3.0; official profile instrument unchanged.

## Student journey

A single visible start leads through nine lifestyle choices, the unchanged 60-item O*NET Short Form, focused Fieldguide follow-ups, pathways, professions, and one current plan. The independent library remains a reading collection. It does not change the guide. Restoring a draft opens the current guide or plan rather than the older report interface.

New guides start in focused mode. All 60 official questions, 12 broad-field activity questions, 12 experience questions, values, and four context questions remain required. Additional setting/process questions expand for fields with endorsed activities or strong full-six profile similarity. Selecting another field gathers its missing context first. Every-field mode remains available. Started drafts retain their earlier full route. No response is fabricated to shorten a route.

Tasks that select every option do not count as focused corroboration. Career support must span multiple evidence domains. Official interests remain unchanged by income, confidence, values, experience, education preferences, or completed activities. Work-context facts and preferences appear separately as connections, tradeoffs and questions to explore.

## Career information completed

All 117 profiles have working-life evidence: 114 O*NET context records and three explicitly sourced qualitative descriptions (financial analysis, EMT and tattoo). Suppressed survey categories are not inferred or renormalized. A regular schedule does not establish daytime hours or job security.

All 19 graduate careers in the finance catalog have named qualifying Texas program references. Clinical psychology also retains a named funded-program example. Additional complete MD and DPT attendance examples fill the route-budget calculation. The optional ACC electrical certificate has a listed-plan tuition calculation, distinct from paid apprenticeship and journeyman licensing. Sources retain different schools, price years, tuition-only versus full attendance scopes, and source-date discrepancies.

All 117 careers now receive three distinct, role-specific exploration steps. Activities use fictional cases, paper models or supervised observation where clinical, electrical or other hazardous work is involved. Completed activities can receive structured “more / less / still curious” feedback, while notes, earlier completions, interests and careers remain saved. Feedback informs the next activity; it does not retroactively change official scores.

## Pay and lifestyle

112 exact-occupation TWC experienced-wage anchors were recovered from the existing reviewed metro tables. Use the selected wage geography, never an undisclosed national substitution. The experienced wage is the mean of the upper-paid two-thirds, not a measured 20-year wage.

For careers without an employer schedule, planning milestones move 45%, 75% and 100% from the entry proxy toward the experienced benchmark, bounded by the local wage distribution. These fractions are modeling choices. They are not fitted longitudinal growth estimates. Suppressed experienced values use the sourced local median. Individual salary outcomes are not predicted.

Austin Police, Austin Fire, AISD teacher placements, Dell residency and Austin ETA apprenticeship retain published schedule examples. Police 5-year and 20-year comparisons use the latest published lower step (2 and 16 years), without inventing intermediate steps. Academy pay is separate from qualified entry, and survives reloads, exports and Developer replay.

Education scenarios separate total cost, grants, cash, study earnings, parent funding, student borrowing, interest and repayment. Borrowing defaults to zero. The selected student loan payment can be linked to Austin expenses once; it does not migrate to a different career. Parent funding does not silently become the student’s payment. Current-dollar school attendance budgets include living where specified; no second living allowance is added on top. Undergraduate academic-year budgets add an explicit editable summer reserve.

Work scenarios can scale paid hours and weeks. Independent work uses receipts minus operating expenses, self-employment taxes, personal income tax and cash savings. Employee wage data is not evidence of independent-business revenue. No QBI deduction or insurance subsidy is assumed. A 10% income reduction and 15% housing increase show stress comparisons, not forecasts.
